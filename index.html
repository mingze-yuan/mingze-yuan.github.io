Mingze Yuan

I am a third-year Ph.D. student in Computer Science at Harvard University, advised by Prof. Na Li and Prof. Quanzheng Li at Harvard Medical School. I also work with Prof. Wengong Jin at Northeastern University.

Previously, I received an M.S. in Data Science (2024) and a B.S. in Statistics, with a second degree in Economics (2021), from Peking University. I was advised by Prof. Bin Dong and was a research intern at Alibaba DAMO Academy (2022–2024).

Research

My research spans generative modeling and large language models (LLMs). I currently focus on (1) self-improving agents for scientific discovery, particularly molecular discovery and spatial biology, and (2) LLM reasoning. My earlier work includes diffusion models and their applications to medical imaging.
